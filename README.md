## About *Friends*

*Friends* is an iconic American sitcom created by David Crane and Marta Kauffman that aired on NBC from September 22, 1994, to May 6, 2004, spanning ten seasons and 236 episodes. Set in Manhattan, New York City, the series follows the personal and professional lives, romantic entanglements, and everyday misadventures of six tight-knit twenty- and thirty-somethings—Rachel Green, Monica Geller, Phoebe Buffay, Joey Tribbiani, Chandler Bing, and Ross Geller—as they navigate adulthood together, often gathered at their favorite neighborhood coffeehouse, Central Perk.

Renowned for its sharp ensemble chemistry, memorable catchphrases, and enduring cultural impact, *Friends* received widespread critical acclaim, earning 62 Primetime Emmy Award nominations and winning Outstanding Comedy Series in 2002 for its eighth season. Decades after its finale, it remains one of the most popular and influential television comedies worldwide.
